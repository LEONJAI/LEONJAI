The Two Ways to Profit from Existing Merchants

Sell through a supplier, or recommend a merchant’s products. Compare who handles the order and how you earn.

Compare the models

Business modelHow you make moneyWho handles customer service?Best platform
01Dropshipping You choose the retail price. For example, a supplier charges $10 and you list the item for $25. When it sells, you pay the supplier, who ships it to the customer. The $15 difference is gross margin before fees, advertising, refunds, and taxes. You. You run the storefront and handle customer questions and problems. The supplier ships the product. Shopify can connect to supplier apps such as AutoDS or Zendrop. App plans and supplier terms vary, so check current fees, shipping, returns, and product quality before choosing.
02Affiliate marketing You share a tracked link to a merchant’s product. If someone buys under the program’s terms, the merchant pays you an agreed commission. You don’t buy or ship inventory. The merchant. The customer checks out with the merchant, which handles payment, delivery, returns, and order support. You should disclose that your link may earn a commission. Your website or social channel, plus a merchant’s own affiliate program or an affiliate network. Each program sets its own approval and payment rules.

These are simplified examples, not guaranteed earnings. Actual costs, margins, commissions, and rules depend on the supplier or affiliate program.

How to choose

Choose dropshipping

If you want to manage a storefront, set prices, coordinate suppliers, and take responsibility for customer support.

Choose affiliate marketing

If you want to publish recommendations and earn commission without managing checkout, inventory, or delivery.
